UML use case • Buying, selling and delivery

Role-specific use cases complement the shared capabilities on page 1. Payment and order status follow separate review steps.

Conditions: GCash proof is required only when GCash is selected. Only suppliers can confirm or complete orders. Location sharing is available for confirmed orders. Buying and selling are represented as goals, not as a sequence diagram.

Use case descriptions and supporting actors

Use case / actorPrecondition and main behaviorResult or alternate path
Register / sign in
Visitor		Create a business or supplier account; authenticate with credentials or a configured social provider.Receive a session and access the role dashboard.
Recover password
Account holder; SMTP service		Request reset email and submit a valid reset token with a new password.Password is updated; invalid or expired tokens fail.
Social sign-in
Visitor; Google / Facebook		Configured provider authenticates the user through OAuth.API resolves the account and redirects to the frontend.
Place order
Buyer		Choose a product, valid quantity, delivery details and payment method. GCash requires a product QR and receipt details.Pending order is saved; supplier receives an update.
Review payment
Supplier		Inspect the order receipt and independently verify received funds.Approve, or reject with a reason; buyer may resubmit.
Confirm / complete
Supplier		GCash must be approved. Confirmation checks available stock; completion requires confirmation.Confirmation reserves stock; completion closes the order.
Track / share location
Buyer; Supplier		Order participant views tracking. Supplier grants browser GPS access and keeps the page open.Locations are stored and shared; maps use OpenStreetMap tiles.
Manage images
Supplier / account holder; Cloudinary		Upload product images, QR image or own profile photo through the API.Hosted image URL is stored. Receipt images use MongoDB instead.
Send feedback / delete account
Visitor / account holder		Submit feedback; authenticated account deletion requires confirmation text.Feedback is stored; deletion removes the account and related records handled by the route.

Scope: no administrator, courier account or automated GCash gateway is implemented. Sources: frontend/src/App.jsx, dashboard and tracking components, backend/server.js, backend/order-status.js, PAYMENTS.md and TRACKING.md. Supporting services above participate in specific use cases; they are not additional user roles.
